# Travel restrictions · Sprint 1

The first release covers France, Spain, the United States, China, Italy,
Türkiye, Mexico, the United Kingdom, Japan and Germany. The research matrix
tracks 35 categories for each destination. Findings in that matrix retain their
native status and supporting sources.

## App behavior

- VisaBrowser shows selected destination-level information for border and
  baggage planning. It appears on a destination result and inside itinerary
  country cards. It is independent of the passport-specific comparison and
  stays out of the saved visa snapshot and eligibility labels.
- TravelPrep shows a Travel restrictions section on the country profile, divided
  into Before you go and During your visit.
- Each note carries a title, scope, source type, source link and date checked.
  Local restrictions identify the relevant location. Aviation authority
  guidance and operating-carrier conditions are individually identified.
  A destination outside the release scope receives no new panel.
- The panels contain only reviewable, actionable notes. Unresolved evidence,
  expired events and ambiguous claims stay in the research records. A panel
  indicates selected guidance and cannot establish that a category or
  destination is clear of restrictions.
- The travel notes are bundled with each app and must render offline wherever
  the corresponding destination profile or visa result is available. Following
  an external source link requires connectivity.
