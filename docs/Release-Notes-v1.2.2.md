# TravelPrep v1.2.2

Sprint 1 adds 106 sourced notes on **Travel restrictions** for France, Spain,
the United States, China, Italy, Türkiye, Mexico, the United Kingdom, Japan and
Germany. Open the panel under the country profile header to browse **Before
you go** and **During your visit**. Each note has a source link, check date and
scope. The panel is bundled for offline use with cached profiles.

- **Before you go** covers selected food, cash and medicine rules, power-bank
  and carrier requirements, and rules for bringing drones into covered
  jurisdictions. It includes Mexico's vaping import restrictions and Japan's
  procedures for regulated medicines.
- **During your visit** covers selected drone operating requirements, local
  conduct and site access. Examples include Sardinian coastal collecting rules,
  reservations for Sugiton in France, and attire at specific religious sites.
  The notes distinguish rules for Mexico's foreign drone operators from the
  requirements for bringing a drone into the country.

The project tracks 350 country-category checks. One is recorded as a verified
conditional restriction, 348 retain scoped or partial evidence, and Mexico's
current local visitor curfew check remains unverified. Research gaps are retained
in the project records. See the [Sprint 1 specification](Travel-Restrictions-Sprint-1-Spec.md).
