Research caveats:
- An aviation rule applies by operating airline, state authority, itinerary and regulatory implementation; never infer a country-wide customs ban.
- The same item can have import, possession, and operation restrictions with different permissions (particularly drones).
- Geographic metadata can be more granular than sovereign-state codes; Macao must remain MO, not be silently copied to CN.
- A source being official does not eliminate need to check effective date, exceptions, translations, route and current applicability.
- Keep source records evidence-first and never imply that an unmentioned country allows a restricted item.

This document is research provenance, *not published travel guidance*. It is intentionally excluded from the app bundle and from VisaBrowser's UI.
