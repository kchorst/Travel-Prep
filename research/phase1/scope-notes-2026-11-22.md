# Scope notes from official sources

- The European Commission's territorial table distinguishes customs, VAT and excise. Åland, the Canary Islands, and several overseas French regions participate in EU customs but have separate VAT and excise treatment. Ceuta and Melilla fall outside EU customs, VAT and excise. The table also gives separate treatment for Monaco, San Marino, Northern Ireland, and Akrotiri and Dhekelia. These distinctions do not establish rules for visas, plant health, pets, drones, or cash. Check the relevant authority before inheriting evidence for any non-core destination. [Commission territorial table](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-directive/how-does-vat-work/territorial-scope_en)
- EASA identifies the participating jurisdictions and maintains links to national aviation authorities. Each state publishes geographic zones that may require permission or prohibit drone operation. The register does not infer local operating clearance or drone import permission from the EASA rule. [EASA member-state resources](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/light/topics/drones-national-aviation-authorities-resources) · [EASA geographical zones](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/the-agency/faqs/geographical-zones-where-i-can-fly)
- The Commission's ETIAS FAQ says the system is not accepting applications. Recheck on each subsequent review because the launch status is time-sensitive. [ETIAS FAQ](https://travel-europe.europa.eu/etias/faq)
- ICAO announced two power banks maximum per passenger and a ban on recharging power banks in flight effective 27 March 2026. Its aviation standard belongs in the aviation evidence index. Confirm the carrier and state rules for each route rather than expanding the standard to country customs findings. [ICAO power-bank announcement](https://www.icao.int/news/new-power-bank-restrictions-will-safeguard-international-aviation)
