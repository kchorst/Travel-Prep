# Destination inventory review

The draft baseline contains all 249 alpha-2 assignments in the installed ISO 3166-1 reference plus 26 project-specific identifiers. The archived destination inventory records 196 matches across both apps and 79 entries present only in VisaBrowser. ISO entries identify countries and areas. They do not resolve travel jurisdiction or local exceptions. The UN M49 list is a statistical cross-reference, with a different purpose from the app inventory.

| Scope to track | Baseline disposition | Separate applicability check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Northern Ireland | UK entry in baseline; no dedicated code | UK and EU sources distinguish goods and certain cash movements. Track the Northern Ireland scope before considering a separate destination entry. |
| Canary Islands | Spain entry; no dedicated code | EU customs applies, while VAT and excise treatment differ. |
| Ceuta and Melilla | Spain entry; no dedicated codes | Outside the EU customs, VAT and excise areas. Track both separately for local and land-border rules. |
| Heligoland and Büsingen | Germany entry; no dedicated codes | Both sit outside EU customs, VAT and excise territory. Check traveler routes separately. |
| Åland and the French overseas regions | Separate codes already present | EU customs and fiscal scopes differ. Look up local procedures and never infer unrestricted entry from the parent country. |
| Akrotiri and Dhekelia | Existing X-AKD | Distinguish external border checks, movements between the bases and Cyprus, and the applicable customs protocol. |
| San Andrés and Leticia | Combined X-SAL | San Andrés archipelago tourist-card evidence is scoped to the archipelago. Review Leticia independently before deciding whether to split the identifier. |
| Jan Mayen | X-JAN plus ISO SJ for Svalbard and Jan Mayen | Review the overlapping identifiers and route-specific rules before assigning inheritance. |
| Ascension and Tristan da Cunha | Individual special entries, plus Saint Helena group and ISO SH | Review jurisdiction-specific entry and separate the umbrella label from legal inheritance. |
| Taiwan and Kosovo | TW and XK already tracked | Keep both research entries. Published scope comes from the authorities applicable to each trip. |

The 275-row count remains the app baseline. Special jurisdictions outside that baseline and overlapping identifiers still require a destination decision and evidence review.
