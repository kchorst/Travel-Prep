# Phase 1 — Official source review: drones and power banks

Checked: 2026-11-22. **Research record only. Not an app release or destination-wide clearance.**

## Singapore drone operation (SG)
- Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore requires registration for aircraft with total weight **over 250 g** before flight. Official source: https://www.caas.gov.sg/unmanned-aircraft/registration-deregistration/
- CAAS requires Broadcast Remote Identification for drones over 250 g operating outdoors, with specified exceptions for permitted operators using FlyItSafe and indoor/enclosed operations. Effective 1 December 2025. Official source: https://www.caas.gov.sg/resources/media-and-publication/newsroom/broadcast-remote-identification-requirement-for-unmanned-aircraft-to-come-into-e/
- Operator and activity permits depend on operation and location. Official source: https://www.caas.gov.sg/unmanned-aircraft/operator-and-activity-permits/
- **Not established by this finding:** general authorization to *import* drones, flights in restricted areas, or permit exemption for every recreational operation.

## UK drone operation (GB)
- UK 2025 amendments extend open-category registration to camera-equipped drones of MTOM **100 g or more** (as specified in amendment), so older international shorthand that drones must always be over 250 g to require registration is inaccurate for UK circumstances.
- Official legal source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1106/made
- Review commencement, operator/pilot ID distinctions, and model-specific conditions against UK CAA before final public wording.
- UK rules must NOT be bulk-inherited from EASA visitor rules.

## ICAO aviation battery framework (GLOBAL-AVIATION)
- ICAO announced an effective date of **27 March 2026** for international flight technical specifications restricting travelers to **two power banks per passenger** and prohibiting recharging power banks during flights.
- Source: https://www.icao.int/news/new-power-bank-restrictions-will-safeguard-international-aviation
- **Not a customs import ban for every country.** Flight-operator policy, checked-vs-cabin carriage, watt-hour limits, local authority guidance, and connection routing need separate verification.
